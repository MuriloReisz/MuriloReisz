## What I do

The part of data work that most projects skip: getting a model or an automation past the notebook and into somebody's Monday morning.

- **AI &amp; machine learning** — churn scoring, demand forecasting, anomaly detection, NLP triage of real inbound text. Calibrated, backtested, and delivered as a worklist someone actually uses.
- **Automation** — month-end closes, reporting pipelines, RPA around the edges. Idempotent jobs that can be re-run without fear.
- **Analytics engineering** — one certified source of truth, modelled properly, so meetings open with decisions instead of reconciling spreadsheets.
- **Analysis &amp; storytelling** — structuring the work so the recommendation is obvious, not just the numbers.

Day job in Apple's CEMEA Sales BPR &amp; Systems team; freelance data and automation work alongside it.
